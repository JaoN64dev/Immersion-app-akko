title: Learn kana
summary: Hiragana and katakana, the two Japanese alphabets. Everything else is built on them.
when: week 1

Japanese is written with three scripts:

| script | used for | example |
|---|---|---|
| **ひらがな** hiragana | grammar endings, particles, native words | たべ**る**, **は**, **の** |
| **カタカナ** katakana | foreign words, names, sound effects | **コーヒー**, **アニメ** |
| **漢字** kanji | the meaning-carrying part of most words | **食**べる, **日本** |

Learn **hiragana first, then katakana**. 46 characters each, and most people can read both in one or two weeks.

- Learn them by **reading**, not by writing: mnemonics (pictures that look like the sound) work well.
- Practise a few minutes a day with a kana drill (search "kana quiz" or "Realkana").
- Don't learn kanji on their own yet. You'll pick them up through words in the next step.

> Avoid romaji (Japanese written in the Latin alphabet) as soon as you can. Reading kana slowly is better than reading romaji fast.

**Done when:** you can read あいうえお… and アイウエオ… without looking anything up, even if you're slow.
