Immersion only teaches you when you understand *some* of it. The fastest way to get there is the most common words, with **Anki** (a free flashcard app that shows each card right before you'd forget it).

1. Install [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) and a **core deck**: a deck of the most frequent words, in order (for example *Kaishi 1.5k*, or any "Core 2k" deck from Anki's shared decks).
2. Do **10–20 new words a day** and **all your reviews**, every day. That's about 15–30 minutes.
3. Read each card's sentence and listen to the audio. You're learning how the word sounds and looks, not only its meaning.

**Connect it to this app:** install the **AnkiConnect** add-on (code `2055492159`), open Anki, then on the [Watch page](/#mined-section) open **Anki → connect** and press **import known words from this deck**. Your mature cards become "known" words here, so subtitles show you exactly what's new.

> Don't add more than you can review. Missing a day of reviews is worse than adding fewer new cards.

**Goal:** 300 known words. Keep the deck going even after you move on, until about 1500 words.
