**AJATT** ("All Japanese All The Time") is a way of learning Japanese described by Khatzumoto on his blog, after he got fluent in about 18 months without classes. Later methods like **Refold** and **MIA** grew out of the same idea. The core of it:

1. **Input is what teaches you.** You pick up a language by hearing and reading huge amounts of it that you *mostly* understand, not by memorising rules. Textbooks and grammar are only there to make the input understandable sooner.
2. **Make Japanese your environment.** Swap the things you already do (anime, YouTube, games, music, podcasts, your phone's language) for Japanese versions. Every hour you would spend on English media can be a Japanese hour.
3. **Fun beats discipline.** Watch and read what you actually enjoy. If something bores you, drop it and find something else. You'll keep doing what's fun for years; you won't keep doing homework.
4. **Not understanding is normal.** 分からなくても大丈夫. At the start you'll understand very little. Comprehension grows slowly from "a few words" to "most of it". Don't stop to translate everything.
5. **A little SRS every day.** Anki makes words stick. Do your reviews every day, keep it short, and spend the rest of your time on Japanese itself.
6. **Speaking comes later.** Output gets much easier after a lot of input. You don't need to force it at the start (but talk if you want to: it's fun).

> AJATT doesn't have to be all or nothing. Every extra hour of Japanese helps. Start with what you can, and grow it.

## How this course works

The steps below go from zero to immersing comfortably. They overlap: you'll keep doing Anki while you immerse, and keep immersing while you learn grammar. Each step has a **when**, and some have goals the app checks for you. Press **mark as done** when you're ready to move on.

| | |
|---|---|
| [観る Watch](/) | anime and dramas with clickable Japanese subtitles |
| [読む Read](/reading.html) | books, articles and manga |
| [聴く Listen](/podcasts.html) | podcasts with transcripts |
| [文法 Grammar](/grammar.html) | short grammar lessons, underlined later in everything you watch |
| [設定 Settings](/settings.html) | your stats, backup and settings |
