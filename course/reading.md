Watching trains your ear. **Reading** trains kanji and vocabulary much faster, because you can stop on every word and the words stay on the page.

## What to read

- **Graded readers**: short books written for learners, by level (search "Tadoku free graded readers"). Start here.
- **NHK News Web Easy**: real news in simple Japanese with furigana.
- **Manga** you like. Slice of life again works best: *Yotsuba&!* is the classic first manga.
- Later: light novels, web novels (小説家になろう), and books you've read in your own language.

## How to read on this app

- On the [Read page](/reading.html), **paste text** or open a **.txt / .epub** file. It's split into clickable words with furigana on the words you don't know, and it remembers where you stopped.
- For **manga**, open the pages or a .cbz file and press **OCR this volume** (it needs `pip install mokuro` once). Then the text in the speech bubbles becomes clickable.
- Turn on **vertical 縦書き** if you want to read like a real Japanese book.

> Don't look up every word. If you get the gist of a sentence, keep going. Look up the words that keep coming back.

**Goals:** 20,000 characters (a couple of graded readers) and 100 manga pages.
