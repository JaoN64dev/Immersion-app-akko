**Sentence mining** means making Anki cards from real Japanese you met yourself: a word, with the sentence you heard it in, the audio and a screenshot. You remember these much better than words from a list, because they come with a scene.

## The i+1 rule

Mine sentences where you understand **everything except one word** ("i+1"). That one word is easy to learn because the rest of the sentence explains it.

On this app, lines with exactly one unknown word get an **i+1** badge in the transcript, and the transcript header shows how many there are.

## How to mine here

1. Set up Anki on the [Watch page](/#mined-section) (**Anki → connect**), choose a deck and a note type, and tick **send to Anki when I mine a word**. Keep **record the line's audio** and **add a screenshot** on.
2. While watching, click the unknown word in an i+1 line and press **+ mine**. The card goes to Anki with the sentence, the line's audio, the word's audio, pitch accent and a screenshot.
3. Mined words become **learning** (yellow). Press **3** on them once you know them.

> Mine **5–10 words a day**, not 50. If your reviews pile up, mine less: the point is to have more time for watching, not more cards.

Once you're mining, you can slow down or stop the core deck. Your own cards are better.

**Goal:** 100 mined words.
