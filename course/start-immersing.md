## When to start

**Listening:** from day one. Put Japanese audio on while you cook, walk or play games, even if you understand nothing. Your ear gets used to the sounds and rhythm long before you understand them.

**Active immersion in this app:** as soon as you can read kana and know a few hundred words, around **300 words and the first grammar lessons**. Before that, clicking every word is more frustrating than useful. After that, it's the best thing you can do.

> Expect to understand very little at first. A comprehension of 30–50% is normal for your first shows. The 理解 % in the corner goes up every week, and that's your progress.

## How to watch on this app

1. On the [Watch page](/), search a show, pick it, and pick a Japanese subtitle file. Then load the episode's video file.
2. Check the **Keys** in the sidebar first: **A / S / D** go to the previous line, replay it, and go to the next one.
3. Words you don't know are underlined in **red**, words you're learning in **yellow**. Hover a line to pause, click a word to look it up.
4. Mark words with the keys **1–4** while hovering: **3** for words you know (they stop being underlined), **4** to ignore names and sound effects.
5. Use the **2nd subtitle** (an English or Portuguese file) only when you're completely lost. Set it to "when paused" so you don't read it all the time.

## What to watch

- **Slice of life** and comedy with everyday speech: *Yotsuba&!*, *Shirokuma Café*, *K-On!*, *Non Non Biyori*, *Flying Witch*.
- Shows you **already watched** in English: you know the story, so you can focus on the Japanese.
- Avoid fantasy and sci-fi with invented words at the start. They're fine later.

**Goal:** 10 hours. After that it gets easier, really.
