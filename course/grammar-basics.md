Grammar is how the words fit together: particles (は, が, を, に), verb endings (〜ます, 〜ている, 〜たい), and so on. You don't need to master it before immersing. You need a **map**, so that when you meet something in a show you think "ah, that's 〜ている" instead of being lost.

**How to use the [grammar page](/grammar.html):**

1. Go through the **N5** lessons in order, a few a day. Read the explanation, click through the examples, do the quiz.
2. Press **mark as learned** when it roughly makes sense. Not when it's perfect: understanding comes from seeing it hundreds of times in real Japanese.
3. Grammar you've studied is **underlined with purple dots** in subtitles and texts. Click an underlined word to see which lesson it's from.

Start with these, they're everywhere:

- [です・ます](/grammar.html#desu-masu), [は and が](/grammar.html#wa-ga), [を](/grammar.html#wo), [に and で](/grammar.html#ni-de)
- [て-form](/grammar.html#te-form), then [〜ている](/grammar.html#te-iru) and [〜てください](/grammar.html#te-kudasai)
- [い and な adjectives](/grammar.html#adjectives), [〜たい](/grammar.html#tai)

> Want more explanation? Tae Kim's *Guide to Japanese* (free online) and Cure Dolly's videos cover the same ground in more depth. Pick one, read it through once, and get back to Japanese.

**Goal:** every N5 lesson marked as learned. N4 comes later, while you immerse.
