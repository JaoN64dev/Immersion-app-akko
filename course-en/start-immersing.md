## When to start

**Listening:** from day one. Put English audio on while you cook, commute or play games, even if you understand little. Your ear gets used to the rhythm long before you understand it.

**Active immersion in this app:** once you know about **300–500 words** and the first grammar lessons. Before that, you'd be clicking nearly every word. After that, it's the best thing you can do.

> Expect around 40–60% comprehension at first. The "understood" % in the corner goes up every week, and that's your progress.

## How to watch on this app

1. On the [Watch page](/), load a video, then its **English subtitles** with **own subtitle file** (.srt / .vtt / .ass). Free subtitles: OpenSubtitles, SubDL.
2. Check the **Keys** in the sidebar: **A / S / D** go to the previous line, replay it, and go to the next one.
3. Words you don't know are underlined in **red**, words you're learning in **yellow**. Hover a line to pause, click a word to look it up.
4. Mark words with the keys **1–4** while hovering: **3** for words you know (the underline goes away), **4** to ignore names and sounds like "uh" or "um".
5. You can load subtitles in your own language as the **2nd subtitle**. Set it to "when paused" and only look when you're really lost.

## What to watch

- **Sitcoms** (short, everyday talk): *Friends*, *The Office*, *Modern Family*, *Brooklyn Nine-Nine*.
- **Animated films**: Pixar and Disney films speak clearly.
- **YouTube**: American creators in a topic you love. Subtitle files can be saved with a tool like yt-dlp.
- Things you **already watched** in your own language: you know the story, so you can focus on the English.

**Goal:** 10 hours. After that it gets easier, really.
