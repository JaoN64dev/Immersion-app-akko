Watching trains your ear. **Reading** grows your vocabulary much faster, because you can stop on any word and the words stay on the page.

## What to read

- **Graded readers**: books written for learners, by level (*Oxford Bookworms*, *Penguin Readers*). Start here.
- **News in Levels** or **VOA Learning English**: simple news.
- Kids' and teen books with lots of dialogue: *Diary of a Wimpy Kid*, *Magic Tree House*.
- Later: novels you like, Reddit, game wikis, anything.

## How to read on this app

- On the [Read page](/reading.html), **paste text** or open a **.txt / .epub** file. Words become clickable, and your place is saved.
- Books where your "understood" % is **90% or more** are the best for reading a lot.

> Don't look up every word. If you get the gist of a sentence, keep going. Look up the words that keep coming back.

**Goal:** 50,000 characters (a few graded readers).
