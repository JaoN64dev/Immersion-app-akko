A big part of everyday English is made of the **2,000–3,000 most common words**. Learn those first and everything else starts making sense faster.

1. Install [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) (free flashcards that show each card right before you'd forget it) and a **frequency deck**: the most common words, in order. Good options: the **NGSL** (New General Service List, about 2,800 words) or a "Core English" shared deck in your own language.
2. Do **10–20 new words a day** and **all your reviews**, every day. That's about 15–30 minutes.
3. Read each card's example sentence and listen to the audio. You're learning how the word sounds and is used, not only what it means.

**Connect it to this app:** install the **AnkiConnect** add-on (code `2055492159`), open Anki, then on the [Watch page](/#mined-section) open **Anki → connect** and press **import known words from this deck**. Your mature cards become "known" words here, so subtitles show you exactly what's new.

> Don't add more than you can review. Missing a day of reviews is worse than adding fewer new cards.

**Goal:** 500 known words. Keep the deck going until about 2,000.
