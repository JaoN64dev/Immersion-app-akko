Grammar is how words fit together. You don't need to master it before immersing. You need a **map**, so that when you hear something in a show you think "ah, that's the present perfect" instead of being lost.

**How to use the [grammar page](/grammar.html):**

1. Go through **A1** in order, a few lessons a day. Read the explanation, click through the examples, do the quiz.
2. Press **mark as learned** when it roughly makes sense. Understanding comes later, from seeing it hundreds of times in real English.
3. Grammar you've studied is **underlined with purple dots** in subtitles and texts. Click an underlined word to see which lesson it's from.

Start with these, they're everywhere:

- [be](/grammar.html#be-verb), [present simple](/grammar.html#present-simple), [do / does / did](/grammar.html#do-questions)
- [present continuous](/grammar.html#present-continuous), [past simple](/grammar.html#past-simple)
- [be going to](/grammar.html#going-to), [will](/grammar.html#will), [present perfect](/grammar.html#present-perfect)

> If you studied English at school, a lot of this will come back quickly. Skim what you know and move on.

**Goal:** every A1 and A2 lesson learned. B1 and up come later, while you immerse.
