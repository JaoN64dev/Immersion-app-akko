Add your own exercises

Exercises are Markdown files. Put your own in the folder below (the guide README.md and a _template.md to copy are already in it). In the desktop app, akko → Open the data folder gets you there.

Each ## heading starts a question: Q: for the question, 男: and 女: for the lines, - for the choices, with * after the right one. Changes show up when you reload this page.

Real recordings: put an audio file (.mp3, .m4a, .wav…) next to your set and write audio: file.mp3 in a question, or audio: file.mp3 1:05-1:48 for just that part of it. It plays instead of the computer voice. Good for the official JLPT samples you download from jlpt.jp, or recordings of a friend reading the scripts. They stay on your computer.
