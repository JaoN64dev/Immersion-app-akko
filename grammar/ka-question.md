---
title: か (questions)
meaning: turns a sentence into a question
level: N5
order: 40
see: masen-ka
---

Put **か** at the end of a polite sentence to make a question. No word order change, no question mark needed.

- 学生です → 学生です**か**。Are you a student?

In casual speech people usually drop か and just raise their voice: 行く？ Inside a sentence, か means "whether": 行く**か**どうか分からない.

## Examples
- これは何ですか。 = What is this?
- 明日、来ますか。 = Are you coming tomorrow?
- 彼が来るかどうか分かりません。 = I don't know whether he'll come.

## Quiz
- トイレはどこです＿＿。 = か | よ | ね
