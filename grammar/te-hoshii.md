**て-form + ほしい** = "I want **someone else** to do". The person takes **に**.

- 友達**に**来**てほしい** (I want my friend to come).
- **〜ないでほしい** = "I don't want you to…": 行か**ないでほしい**.

> 〜たい is what *you* want to do. 〜てほしい is what you want *others* to do.

## Examples
- もう少し静かにしてほしい。 = I want you to be a bit quieter.
- 誰にも言わないでほしい。 = I don't want you to tell anyone.

## Quiz
- 母に元気でい＿＿＿ほしい。 = て | たい | に
