**は** (written は, said *wa*) marks the **topic**: "as for X…". **が** marks the **subject**: the one who does the action or has the quality.

- Use **は** for something already known, or to compare: 私**は**学生です (as for me, I'm a student).
- Use **が** for new information or to point at exactly *who*: 誰**が**来ましたか → 田中さん**が**来ました.
- After question words (誰, 何, どれ) as subject, use **が**, never は.
- With 好き, 上手, 分かる, ある/いる, the thing liked, understood or existing takes **が**: 猫**が**好きです.

> A good start: は answers "what about it?", が answers "who / which one?".

## Examples
- 私は日本人じゃありません。 = I'm not Japanese.
- 誰が窓を開けましたか。 = Who opened the window?
- 象は鼻が長い。 = Elephants have long trunks. (As for elephants, the trunk is long.)
- 犬が好きです。 = I like dogs.

- 誰＿＿来ましたか。 = が | は | を
- 私＿＿アンナです。はじめまして。 = は | が | を
