**noun / stem + っぽい** = "-ish, looks like, tends to". Casual.

- Some are one word in the dictionary (忘れっぽい, 熱っぽい), so only the っぽい that stands apart is underlined.
- 子供**っぽい** (childish), 白**っぽい** (whitish), 忘れ**っぽい** (forgetful), 怒り**っぽい** (quick to get angry).
- Also "kind of seems": 風邪**っぽい** (I think I'm catching a cold).

## Examples
- 彼は時々子供っぽいことを言う。 = He sometimes says childish things.
- 彼の話し方はちょっと子供っぽい。 = The way he talks is a bit childish.
- ちょっと風邪っぽいから、早く寝る。 = I feel like I'm getting a cold, so I'll go to bed early.

## Quiz
- あの人は怒り＿＿＿から、気をつけて。 = っぽい | らしい | みたい
