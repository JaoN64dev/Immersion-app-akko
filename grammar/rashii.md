Two uses:

1. **Apparently / I hear** (from something you heard or read, less sure than そうだ): 彼は会社を辞める**らしい**.
2. **Typical of, like a real …** (after a noun): 男**らしい** (manly), 春**らしい**天気 (proper spring weather), 君**らしくない** (not like you).

## Examples
- 明日は雪が降るらしいよ。 = Apparently it's going to snow tomorrow.
- あの二人、付き合っているらしい。 = Apparently those two are dating.
- 今日は春らしい暖かい日ですね。 = It's a nice warm, spring-like day today.

## Quiz
- そんなことを言うなんて、君＿＿＿ないね。 = らしく | そう | みたい
