**はず** = what you **expect** based on reasons: "it should be / it's supposed to be".

- 彼はもう着いている**はずだ**。He should have arrived by now.
- **〜はずがない**: "there's no way" (strong denial): そんな**はずがない**!
- **〜はずだった**: "was supposed to (but didn't)".

> はず is about expectation, not duty. For "you should (do it)", use べき.

## Examples
- 荷物は明日届くはずです。 = The package should arrive tomorrow.
- 鍵はここに置いたはずなのに、ない。 = I'm sure I put the key here, but it's gone.
- 彼が嘘をつくはずがない。 = There's no way he'd lie.

## Quiz
- 三時に出発したから、もう着いている＿＿＿だ。 = はず | べき | ばかり
- そんな＿＿はない！(no way!) = はず | べき | まま
