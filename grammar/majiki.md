**dictionary form + まじき + noun** = "a(n) X that should never be done (by someone in that position)". Very formal. Often 〜にあるまじき (unbecoming of).

> 教師にある**まじき**行為。Behaviour unbecoming of a teacher.

## Examples
- それは政治家として許すまじき発言だ。 = That's a remark a politician should never be forgiven for.
- 子供を傷つけるなど、親にあるまじきことだ。 = Hurting a child is something a parent should never do.

## Quiz
- 医者にある＿＿＿ミスだ。 = まじき | べき | べからず
