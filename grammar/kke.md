**〜っけ** at the end of a sentence = "what was it again? / didn't you say…?". You're trying to remember, or checking something you half know. Casual.

- 名前、何だった**っけ**？
- 明日は休みだ**っけ**？

## Examples
- あの映画のタイトル、何だっけ？ = What was that film's title again?
- 会議は何時からだっけ？ = What time does the meeting start again?

## Quiz
- えっと、彼の誕生日はいつだった＿＿？ = っけ | っぽい | ね
