**plain form / noun + とはいえ** = "although it's true that X, (still)". Also at the start of a sentence: **とはいえ、** (that said,).

> 春**とはいえ**、まだ寒い。Although it's spring, it's still cold.

## Examples
- 休みとはいえ、やることがたくさんある。 = Even though it's a day off, I have lots to do.
- 便利になった。とはいえ、問題もある。 = It's become convenient. That said, there are problems too.
