- **dictionary form + 一方だ** = "keeps getting more and more" (one direction, often bad): 物価は上がる**一方だ**.
- **〜一方(で)** = "while, on the other hand": 便利な**一方で**、危険もある.

## Examples
- この町の人口は減る一方だ。 = This town's population just keeps falling.
- 仕事は楽しい一方で、ストレスも多い。 = Work is fun, but on the other hand it's stressful.

## Quiz
- 最近、仕事が増える＿＿＿だ。 = 一方 | まま | うち
