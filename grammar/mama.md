**〜まま** = something stays the same, while normally it would change.

- た-form: 窓を開けた**まま**寝た (slept with the window left open).
- Noun の: 靴の**まま**入らないで (don't come in with your shoes on).
- **そのまま** = as it is.

## Examples
- 電気をつけたまま寝てしまった。 = I fell asleep with the light on.
- 昔のままの町並みが残っている。 = The streets have stayed as they were long ago.
