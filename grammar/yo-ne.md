Two endings that carry feeling more than meaning:

- **よ**: you give new information or insist. 美味しい**よ**! (It's tasty, trust me!)
- **ね**: you look for agreement, or share a feeling. 美味しい**ね**。(It's tasty, isn't it?)
- **よね**: "…right?", checking something you think is true.

## Examples
- この映画、面白いよ。 = This film is good, you know.
- 今日は暑いですね。 = It's hot today, isn't it?
- 明日は休みだよね？ = Tomorrow's a day off, right?

## Quiz
- (agreeing with a friend) 本当に寒い＿＿。 = ね | よ | か
- (telling someone something they don't know) あ、財布を落としました＿＿！ = よ | ね | か
