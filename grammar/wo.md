**を** (said *o*) marks the **object**: the thing that is eaten, read, bought, watched…

> 本**を**読む: read *a book*. パン**を**食べる: eat *bread*.

を is also used for the place you **go through or leave**: 公園**を**散歩する (walk through the park), 家**を**出る (leave the house).

## Examples
- 毎日日本語を勉強します。 = I study Japanese every day.
- 水を飲んでください。 = Please drink some water.
- 橋を渡る。 = Cross the bridge.
