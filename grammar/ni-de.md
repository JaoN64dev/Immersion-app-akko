---
title: に and で
meaning: に = to / at a time / exists at, で = where it happens / with
level: N5
order: 30
see: wo, aru-iru
---

Both can translate as "at" or "in", but they do different jobs.

| particle | job | example |
|---|---|---|
| に | where something **is** (with ある/いる) | 部屋**に**猫がいる |
| に | where you **go to** | 学校**に**行く |
| に | a **point in time** | 7時**に**起きる |
| に | the person you give to / meet | 友達**に**会う |
| で | where an **action happens** | 図書館**で**勉強する |
| で | the **tool or means** | 電車**で**行く, 箸**で**食べる |

> Ask: does something *exist* there or *move* there (に), or does something *happen* there (で)?

## Examples
- 駅の前に本屋があります。 = There is a bookshop in front of the station.
- 駅の前で友達を待ちます。 = I'll wait for my friend in front of the station.
- 日曜日に映画を見ました。 = I saw a film on Sunday.
- バスで会社に行きます。 = I go to work by bus.

## Quiz
- 公園＿＿テニスをします。 = で | に | を
- 机の上＿＿ペンがあります。 = に | で | を
- 毎朝6時＿＿起きます。 = に | で | が
