Your words

Your word list, Anki settings and language come from akko by themselves while akko is running (the desktop app or npm start). Words you mark, cards you mine and the time you watch go back to akko: they show in its Stats and 復習, and count for today and your streak. If akko is closed, they wait here until it's back.

Anki

Cards go through akko to Anki (with the AnkiConnect add-on, 2055492159), and into akko's mined list. These settings come from akko's own Anki settings; you can also set them here. In the player's akko ▾ menu you can choose to send cards only to akko.

Translation

On YouTube: the video's own subtitles in that language when it has them, otherwise YouTube's translation.
