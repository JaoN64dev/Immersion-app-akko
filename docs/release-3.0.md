# akko 3.0

Everything still runs on your own computer: no account needed, nothing uploaded, no paid service.

## Learn any language

- **Any language** you have a Yomitan dictionary for (Settings → Dictionaries, or *add from a link*).
  Korean, Mandarin and Cantonese have one-click packs.
- The interface in **nine languages**: English, 日本語, Português, Español, Deutsch, Français, Italiano,
  Русский, 中文, 한국어.

## Know what to read next

- **Your week, planned**: texts, videos, podcasts and manga ordered by how much of them you already know.
- The library shows **how much of each text you know before you open it**, and the words worth learning first.
- **Stats**: your year day by day, how far you are from the next level, and how well you remember
  words by where you mined them.

## Watch

- **Shadowing** (`R`): hear a line, say it after, and see your melody next to the original's.
- **Subtitles from the video's audio**, made by Whisper on your computer. OpenAI's whisper now works
  even without ffmpeg.
- **Magnet links and .torrent files**: paste one (or nyaa's download link) and the episode streams to
  the player. Season torrents get the episode list, "next episode", and the next one downloaded
  ahead. While an episode plays, akko shares pieces like any torrent client (100 KB/s at most);
  what you download and share is up to you.

## Read

- **Web pages** (Japanese sites in any encoding) and **PDFs** straight into the reader.
- **Read aloud**, with each word lit up as it's said, and a word list per book (.tsv).
- **Look up a word** from the menu or with `/`: type it, paste it, or **draw** a kanji.
- **Kanji stroke order**: tap a kanji in the word popup.

## Understand

- **Explain** in the word popup: the whole sentence word by word, with its grammar. With Ollama
  installed, a local language model also writes an explanation (Settings → Local assistant).

## Anki

- **Anki decks (.apkg)** without AnkiConnect: your mined words with pictures, line audio, word audio
  and their 復習 schedule.
- **Known words from an Anki file** (.apkg / .colpkg).
- New card fields: frequency, FreqSort, the page a word came from.

## Fixed

- Pages in Shift_JIS / EUC-JP no longer turn into mojibake in the reader.
- Read-aloud stops cleanly and keeps the words it met.
- 〜てある is no longer suggested for である.
