Learning

The language you're studying. Each one keeps its own known words, lessons, course and progress, so you can switch back and forth.

English subtitles

The Watch page can search OpenSubtitles.com for English subtitles. It needs a free API key: make an account there, then in your profile open API consumers and create one. Without logging in you get about 5 downloads a day; with your account, 20. The key and login are kept on this computer only (the password is not saved).

    YouTube and Netflix

    With the akko companion browser extension (Chrome or Edge), YouTube videos, Netflix titles and other video sites with subtitles in the language you learn get akko's clickable subtitles: the dictionary, your word colours, and "+ mine" to Anki. Words you mark and mine while watching come back here, and the time counts for today and your streak.

      Every 3 days, a backup is saved by itself on this computer; the last 10 are kept.
