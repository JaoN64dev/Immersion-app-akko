English subtitles

The Watch page can search OpenSubtitles.com for English subtitles. It needs a free API key: make an account there, then in your profile open API consumers and create one. Without logging in you get about 5 downloads a day; with your account, 20. The key and login are kept on this computer only (the password is not saved).

      how your data is kept

      What's kept: your e-mail; your password as a hash (never the password itself); your word list; your mined words (the word, its reading and meaning, the sentence and its translation, where and when it's from) and their recordings (the line's audio and a picture); the names of the computers you signed in on, and when each was last used.

      Where: on Cloudflare (a database and file storage), run for akko. It's only used to keep your computers in step: no ads, no tracking, never sold or shared.

      Yours: download my data gives you all of it; delete the account removes all of it, recordings too. Without an account, nothing leaves your computer.

      YouTube and Netflix

      With the akko companion browser extension (Chrome or Edge), YouTube videos, Netflix titles and other video sites with subtitles in the language you learn get akko's clickable subtitles: the dictionary, your word colours, and "+ mine" to Anki. Words you mark and mine while watching come back here, and the time counts for today and your streak.

      …

      1. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
      2. Click Load unpacked and pick akko's extension folder:
      3. Open a YouTube video with subtitles in the language you learn. akko needs to be open while you watch.

      The folder comes with akko (and is updated with it: after an update, click the extension's reload arrow in chrome://extensions). It's also on each release as akko-companion.zip.
