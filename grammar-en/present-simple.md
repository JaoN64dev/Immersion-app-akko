The **present simple** is for things that happen regularly, and for facts. It's *not* for things happening right now.

- I **play** tennis on Sundays.
- With **he / she / it**, add **-s**: she **plays**, he **works**, it **rains**.
- After -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o add **-es**: watch → watch**es**, go → go**es**.
- Consonant + y → **-ies**: study → stud**ies**.
- **have** → **has**.

> For something happening now, use the present continuous: I'**m playing**.

## Examples
- I drink coffee every morning.
- My brother works at a bank.
- The store opens at nine.

## Quiz
- She ___ English every day. (study) = studies | study | studys
- He ___ two cats. (have) = has | haves | have
