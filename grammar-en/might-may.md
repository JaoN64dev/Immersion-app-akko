For "maybe": **might / may / could + verb**.

- It **might rain** later.
- She **may be** at home.
- That **could be** true.
- Negative: **might not / may not**. (*could not* means "it's impossible".)

> **may** is also a polite way to ask permission: **May** I come in?

## Examples
- I might go to the party, but I'm not sure.
- He may be late because of the traffic.
- That could be a problem.

## Quiz
- Take an umbrella. It ___ rain. = might | must | should
