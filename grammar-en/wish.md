After **wish**, the tense moves back, like in conditionals.

| about | form | example |
|---|---|---|
| now | wish + **past** | I **wish** I **were** taller. |
| the past (regret) | wish + **had + past participle** | I **wish** I **had studied** harder. |
| someone annoying you | wish + **would** | I **wish** you **would** stop talking. |

**If only** is a stronger wish: **If only** I had more time!

## Examples
- I wish I could speak English fluently.
- I wish I had brought my umbrella.
- If only it would stop raining!

## Quiz
- I wish I ___ more money. (now) = had | have | will have
