They say how often something happens.

| 100% | | | | 0% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **always** | **usually** | **often** | **sometimes** | **never** |

Position: **before** most verbs, **after** be.

- I **usually get up** at seven.
- She **is always** late.

## Examples
- I often go jogging in the morning.
- He is never on time.
- We sometimes eat out on Fridays.

## Quiz
- Which is right? = I always drink | I drink always | Always I drink
