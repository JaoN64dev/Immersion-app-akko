**used to + verb** = it was true in the past, but it isn't now.

- I **used to** play the piano.
- Negative: I **didn't use to** like coffee.
- Question: **Did** you **use to** live here?

> Don't mix it up with **be used to + -ing** (= be familiar with). See the B2 lesson.

## Examples
- I used to live in Texas.
- There used to be a park here.
- He didn't use to drink coffee.

## Quiz
- When I was a kid, I used to ___ a lot of cartoons. = watch | watching | watched
