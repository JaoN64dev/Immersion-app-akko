**have / get + thing + past participle** = someone else does it for you (usually a service).

- I **had** my hair **cut**.
- I need to **get** my car **fixed**.

Making people do things:
- **make** + person + verb = force: My mom **made me clean** my room.
- **let** + person + verb = allow: **Let me** help.

## Examples
- I had my hair cut yesterday.
- We need to get the roof repaired.
- My parents never let me stay out late.

## Quiz
- I'm going to have my phone ___ . = repaired | repair | repairing
