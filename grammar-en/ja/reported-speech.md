---
title: 間接話法 (said that / told me)
meaning: 〜と言った
---

人が言ったことを伝えるとき、時制を一つ過去にずらすのが基本です。

| 本人の言葉 | 伝えるとき |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired." | She said (that) she **was** tired. |
| "I **will** help." | He said he **would** help. |
| "I **saw** it." | She said she **had seen** it. |

- **say** は相手なし: He **said** that... / **tell** は相手が必要: He **told me** that...
- 命令・お願い: She **told me to** wait. / He **asked me to** help.
- 質問: She **asked if** I was OK.

## Examples
- She said that she was busy. = 彼女は忙しいと言いました。
- He told me that he loved me. = 彼は私を愛していると言いました。
- My boss asked me to stay late. = 上司に残業を頼まれました。
