---
title: 関係代名詞 (who / which / that)
meaning: 〜する人・〜するもの
---

名詞のあとに **who / which / that** を置いて、どんな人・ものかを説明します。

| | 使う語 |
|---|---|
| 人 | **who** (that) |
| もの・動物 | **which** (that) |
| 「〜の」 | **whose** |

- The woman **who** lives next door is a nurse.
- I lost the bag **which** I bought yesterday.
- 目的語のときは省略できます: The movie (that) we saw was great.

## Examples
- I have a friend who speaks five languages. = 5か国語を話す友達がいます。
- This is the book which changed my life. = これが私の人生を変えた本です。
- That's the man whose car was stolen. = あれが車を盗まれた男性です。
