---
title: 倒置 (Never have I…)
meaning: 否定の言葉を前に出して強調する
---

否定的な言葉を文の頭に出すと、そのあとが **疑問文の語順** になります。書き言葉やスピーチで強調するときの形です。

| ふつう | 倒置 |
|---|---|
| I have never seen such a thing. | **Never have I** seen such a thing. |
| He not only sings, but also dances. | **Not only does he** sing, but he also dances. |
| As soon as I arrived, it started to rain. | **No sooner had I** arrived than it started to rain. |

## Examples
- Never have I been so embarrassed. = こんなに恥ずかしかったことはない。
- Not only did she win, but she also broke the record. = 彼女は勝っただけでなく、記録も破った。
- Rarely do we see such talent. = これほどの才能はめったに見られない。
