---
title: 比較級 (-er / more ... than)
meaning: 〜より…だ
---

2つを比べるときは **比較級 + than**。

| 形容詞 | 比較級 |
|---|---|
| 短い語 | tall → tall**er**, big → big**ger**, easy → eas**ier** |
| 長い語 | **more** beautiful, **more** expensive |
| 不規則 | good → **better**, bad → **worse**, far → **further** |

> Tokyo is **bigger than** Osaka. / This is **more expensive than** that.
> 同じくらい: **as** tall **as**

## Examples
- My sister is older than me. = 姉は私より年上です。
- This book is more interesting than the movie. = この本は映画より面白いです。
- Today is better than yesterday. = 今日は昨日よりいいです。
