**There is / There are** says that something exists, or is somewhere.

- One thing: **There is** a cat on the sofa. (spoken: **There's**)
- More than one: **There are** two books on the desk.
- Question: **Is there** a bank near here?
- Past: There **was** / There **were**.

## Examples
- There's a gas station on the corner.
- There are a lot of people at the station.
- There was a big storm last night.

## Quiz
- There ___ three apples in the box. = are | is | be
- ___ there a hospital near here? = Is | Are | Does
