**be** says what something is, or where it is. Its form depends on the subject.

| subject | present | past |
|---|---|---|
| I | **am** | was |
| he / she / it | **is** | was |
| you / we / they | **are** | were |

- Negative: add **not**: I'm **not** tired.
- Question: put **be** first: **Are** you ready?
- In speech it's usually short: I'm, you're, he's, it's, we're, isn't, aren't.

## Examples
- I'm a student.
- She is in the kitchen.
- We aren't hungry.
- Are you from Brazil?

## Quiz
- They ___ my friends. = are | is | am
- I ___ at home yesterday. = was | were | am
- Short form of "he is"? = he's
