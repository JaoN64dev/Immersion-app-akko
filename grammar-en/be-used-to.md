**be used to + noun / verb-ing** = something is normal for you now. **get used to** = it becomes normal.

- I'**m used to** waking up early.
- You'll **get used to** it.

> Different from **used to + verb** (I used to live there = in the past)! Here, after *to* comes **-ing** or a noun.

## Examples
- I'm used to living alone.
- It took me a year to get used to the food.
- She isn't used to driving on the right.

## Quiz
- I'm used to ___ in English. = working | work | worked
- In the past: I used to ___ in Ohio. = live | living | lived
