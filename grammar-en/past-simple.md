The **past simple** is for finished things in the past.

- Regular verbs add **-ed**: play → play**ed**, live → live**d**, study → stud**ied**, stop → sto**pped**.
- Irregular verbs have to be learned: go → **went**, eat → **ate**, see → **saw**, have → **had**, buy → **bought**, take → **took**.
- Questions and negatives use **did** + plain verb: **Did** you **go**? / I **didn't go**.

Often with: yesterday, last week, two days **ago**, in 2020.

> -ed has three sounds: /t/ (walked), /d/ (played), /ɪd/ (wanted).

## Examples
- I went to New York last summer.
- We watched a movie yesterday.
- She bought a new phone.

## Quiz
- I ___ pizza last night. (eat) = ate | eated | eaten
- Past of "study"? = studied
