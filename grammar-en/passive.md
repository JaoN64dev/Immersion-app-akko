**be + past participle** = the passive. Use it when what happened matters more than who did it.

| tense | example |
|---|---|
| present | English **is spoken** here. |
| past | This bridge **was built** in 1932. |
| present perfect | My bike **has been stolen**! |
| future | It **will be finished** tomorrow. |

Say who did it with **by**: The book was written **by** Mark Twain.

> tired, interested, excited… have the same shape (be + past participle), so they're underlined too.

## Examples
- This bridge was built in 1932.
- English is spoken by about 1.5 billion people.
- The meeting has been canceled.

## Quiz
- The letter ___ sent yesterday. = was | is | has
- Smoking is not ___ here. = allowed | allow | allowing
