Putting a negative word first makes the rest use **question word order**. It's formal and emphatic, common in writing and speeches.

| normal | inverted |
|---|---|
| I have never seen such a thing. | **Never have I** seen such a thing. |
| He not only sings, but he also dances. | **Not only does he** sing, but he also dances. |
| As soon as I arrived, it started to rain. | **No sooner had I** arrived than it started to rain. |

## Examples
- Never have I been so embarrassed.
- Not only did she win, but she also broke the record.
- Rarely do we see such talent.

- Never ___ I heard such nonsense. = have | I have | did I
