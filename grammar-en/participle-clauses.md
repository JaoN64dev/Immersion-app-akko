In writing, you can drop the subject and the linking word and start with **-ing** or a past participle.

- **Walking** home, I met an old friend. (= while I was walking)
- **Having finished** the report, she went home. (= after she had finished)
- **Written** in 1950, the book is still popular. (= it was written)

## Examples
- Having finished his homework, he went out to play.
- Not having eaten all day, I was starving.
