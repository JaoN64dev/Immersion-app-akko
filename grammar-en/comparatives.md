To compare two things: **comparative + than**.

| adjective | comparative |
|---|---|
| short | tall → tall**er**, big → big**ger**, easy → eas**ier** |
| long | **more** beautiful, **more** expensive |
| irregular | good → **better**, bad → **worse**, far → **farther / further** |

> Texas is **bigger than** California. / This one is **more expensive than** that one.
> The same: **as** tall **as**.

## Examples
- My sister is older than me.
- This book is more interesting than the movie.
- Today is better than yesterday.

## Quiz
- A car is ___ than a bike. (fast) = faster | more fast | fastest
- Comparative of "good"? = better
