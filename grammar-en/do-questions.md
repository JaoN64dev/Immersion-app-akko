Verbs other than **be** use **do / does / did** for questions and negatives. The main verb goes back to its plain form.

| | question | negative |
|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | **Do** you like sushi? | I **don't** like it. |
| he / she / it | **Does** she work here? | She **doesn't** work here. |
| past (everyone) | **Did** you see it? | I **didn't** see it. |

> After does / did, no -s and no -ed: ~~Does she works?~~ → **Does she work?**

## Examples
- Do you have any brothers or sisters?
- He doesn't eat meat.
- Did you sleep well?

## Quiz
- ___ she live in Chicago? = Does | Do | Is
- I didn't ___ the news. = see | saw | seen
