title: Verb + -ing or verb + to (enjoy doing, want to do)
meaning: which form comes after a verb
level: A2
order: 90
detect: ~like|~love|~hate|~enjoy|~mind|~finish|~stop|~keep + [ing]
detect: would|'d + like + to
detect: ~want|~need|~hope|~decide|~plan + to + [base]
---

When one verb follows another, the first verb decides the form.

| + -ing | + to + verb | both |
|---|---|---|
| enjoy, finish, mind, stop, keep | want, need, hope, decide, plan | like, love, hate, start |

- I **enjoy cooking**. / I **want to travel**.
- Polite "I want": **I'd like to** + verb: I**'d like to** order, please.

## Examples
- I really enjoy reading comics.
- She wants to be a doctor.
- I'd like to reserve a table for two.

## Quiz
- Do you mind ___ the window? = opening | to open | open
- I decided ___ Spanish. = to learn | learning | learn
