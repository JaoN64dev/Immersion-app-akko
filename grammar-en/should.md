**should + verb** gives advice: it's a good idea.

- You **should** see a doctor.
- Negative: **shouldn't**: You **shouldn't** stay up so late.
- Asking for advice: What **should** I do?

## Examples
- You should try this cake.
- We shouldn't be late.
- Should I bring anything?

## Quiz
- You look tired. You should ___ . = rest | resting | to rest
