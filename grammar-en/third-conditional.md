To imagine a different past (often regret):

> **If** I **had studied** harder, I **would have passed** the exam.

- if part: **had + past participle**
- result: **would have + past participle** (or could have / might have)
- In speech: If I'**d known**, I'**d have told** you. (sounds like "I'd'a told you")

## Examples
- If I had known, I would have helped you.
- If we'd left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight.
- She would have come if you had invited her.

## Quiz
- If I ___ the alarm, I wouldn't have been late. = had heard | heard | would hear
