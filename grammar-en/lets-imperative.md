- **Commands**: start with the plain verb: **Sit** down. **Be** quiet.
- Negative commands: **Don't** + verb: **Don't** touch it!
- Softer: add **please**: **Please** come in.
- **Let's + verb** = a suggestion for "you and me": **Let's** go!

## Examples
- Let's take a break.
- Let's meet at the station.
- Please close the door.

## Quiz
- ___ eat out tonight! = Let's | Let | Lets to
- ___ be late! (a command) = Don't | Not | No
