**will + verb**:

1. **Predicting**: It **will** be cold tomorrow.
2. **Deciding right now**: I'**ll** call you later.
3. **Promises and offers**: I **won't** tell anyone.
4. **Requests**: **Will** you marry me?

Negative: **won't** (= will not). In speech: I'll, you'll, it'll.

## Examples
- I'll help you with your bags.
- Don't worry, it won't hurt.
- I think she will win.

## Quiz
- "Someone is at the door." "I ___ get it." = 'll | am | going
- Short form of "will not"? = won't
