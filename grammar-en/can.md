**can + verb** = be able to. The verb stays plain, and there's no -s: she **can swim** (not *cans*, not *can swims*).

- Negative: **can't** (cannot): I **can't** drive.
- Asking for help: **Can** you help me?
- Permission: You **can** sit here.
- Past ability: **could**: I **could** read when I was four.
- **Could** is also a more polite request: **Could** you open the door?

## Examples
- She can speak three languages.
- I can't find my keys.
- Can you open the window?

## Quiz
- He can ___ the piano. = play | plays | playing
- I ___ hear you. It's too loud. = can't | don't | won't
