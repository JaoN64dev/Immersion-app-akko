**had + past participle** = something that happened **before** another past moment.

> When I got to the station, the train **had** already **left**.

Order: ① the train left (had left) → ② I got to the station (got).

## Examples
- I had never seen snow before I moved to Chicago.
- She had already eaten when I called.
- We'd just arrived when it started raining.

## Quiz
- When we arrived, the movie ___ already started. = had | has | was
