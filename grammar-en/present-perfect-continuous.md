meaning: something that started in the past and is still going
level: B1

detect: have|has + [pronoun] + been + [ing]

**have / has been + verb-ing** = an action that started in the past and is **still going on** (or just stopped).

- I'**ve been waiting** for an hour!
- How long **have** you **been studying** English?
- You're wet. **Have** you **been swimming**?

## Examples
- I've been studying English for three years.
- It's been raining all day.
- How long have you been living here?

## Quiz
- She has been ___ all morning. = working | worked | work
