To tell what someone said, the tense usually moves one step back.

| they said | you report |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired." | She said (that) she **was** tired. |
| "I **will** help." | He said he **would** help. |
| "I **saw** it." | She said she **had seen** it. |

- **say** has no person after it: He **said** that… / **tell** needs a person: He **told me** that…
- Orders and requests: She **told me to** wait. / He **asked me to** help.
- Questions: She **asked if** I was OK.

## Examples
- She said that she was busy.
- He told me that he loved me.
- My boss asked me to stay late.

## Quiz
- He ___ me that he was sick. = told | said | spoke
- "I am tired." → She said she ___ tired. = was | is | be
