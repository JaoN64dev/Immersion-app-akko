title: Relative clauses (who / which / that)
meaning: adding information about a noun

**who / which / that** after a noun tell us which person or thing.

| | word |
|---|---|
| people | **who** (that) |
| things, animals | **which** (that) |
| possession | **whose** |

- The woman **who** lives next door is a nurse.
- I lost the bag **which** I bought yesterday.
- When it's the object, you can drop it: The movie (that) we saw was great.

## Examples
- I have a friend who speaks five languages.
- This is the book which changed my life.
- That's the man whose car was stolen.

## Quiz
- The girl ___ is singing is my sister. = who | which | whose
- I like stories ___ have happy endings. = which | who | whose
