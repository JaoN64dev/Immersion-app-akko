**modal + have + past participle** talks about the past.

| | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| **should have** | it was a good idea, but I didn't | I **should have** studied more. |
| **could have** | it was possible, but it didn't happen | You **could have** told me! |
| **must have** | I'm sure it happened | He **must have** forgotten. |
| **might have** | maybe it happened | She **might have** missed the bus. |

In speech: **should've / could've** (they sound like "should of").

## Examples
- I should have listened to you.
- She must have been tired.
- You could've called me!

## Quiz
- (a regret) I ___ have bought that jacket. = should | must | can
- The ground is wet. It ___ have rained. = must | should | could not
