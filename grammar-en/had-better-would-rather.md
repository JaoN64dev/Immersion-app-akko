- **had better + verb** = strong advice, almost a warning (or something bad happens): You'**d better** hurry, or you'll miss the bus.
- **would rather + verb** = prefer: I'**d rather** stay home **than** go out.

Both are usually short in speech: **'d**.

## Examples
- You'd better not tell anyone.
- I'd rather walk than take the bus.
- We had better leave now.
