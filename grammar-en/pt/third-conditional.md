---
title: Third conditional (if I had known, I would have…)
meaning: imaginar um passado diferente
---

Para imaginar um passado diferente (muitas vezes arrependimento): o nosso "se eu tivesse ..., teria ...".

> **If** I **had studied** harder, I **would have passed** the exam.

- Parte do if: **had + particípio**
- Resultado: **would have + particípio** (ou could have / might have)
- Na fala: If I'**d known**, I'**d have told** you. (soa como "I'd'a told you")

## Examples
- If I had known, I would have helped you. = Se eu soubesse, teria te ajudado.
- If we'd left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight. = Se tivéssemos saído mais cedo, não teríamos perdido o voo.
- She would have come if you had invited her. = Ela teria vindo se você a tivesse convidado.
