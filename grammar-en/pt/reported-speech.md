---
title: Discurso indireto (she said that…)
meaning: contar o que alguém disse
---

Para contar o que alguém disse, o tempo verbal costuma voltar um passo.

| a pessoa disse | você conta |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired." | She said (that) she **was** tired. |
| "I **will** help." | He said he **would** help. |
| "I **saw** it." | She said she **had seen** it. |

- **say** não leva pessoa depois: He **said** that… / **tell** precisa de pessoa: He **told me** that…
- Ordens e pedidos: She **told me to** wait. / He **asked me to** help.
- Perguntas: She **asked if** I was OK.

## Examples
- She said that she was busy. = Ela disse que estava ocupada.
- He told me that he loved me. = Ele me disse que me amava.
- My boss asked me to stay late. = Meu chefe me pediu para ficar até mais tarde.
