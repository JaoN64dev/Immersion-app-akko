---
title: Perguntas e negativas com do / does / did
meaning: como perguntar e negar com a maioria dos verbos
---

Os verbos (menos **be**) usam **do / does / did** para perguntas e negativas. O verbo principal volta para a forma simples.

| | pergunta | negativa |
|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | **Do** you like sushi? | I **don't** like it. |
| he / she / it | **Does** she work here? | She **doesn't** work here. |
| passado (todos) | **Did** you see it? | I **didn't** see it. |

> Depois de does / did, nada de -s nem -ed: ~~Does she works?~~ → **Does she work?**

## Examples
- Do you have any brothers or sisters? = Você tem irmãos?
- He doesn't eat meat. = Ele não come carne.
- Did you sleep well? = Você dormiu bem?
