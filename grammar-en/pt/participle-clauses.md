---
title: Orações reduzidas (Having finished…)
meaning: juntar ideias com -ing / -ed (formal)
---

Na escrita, dá para tirar o sujeito e a palavra de ligação e começar com **-ing** ou um particípio (como o nosso gerúndio: "tendo terminado...").

- **Walking** home, I met an old friend. (= while I was walking)
- **Having finished** the report, she went home. (= after she had finished)
- **Written** in 1950, the book is still popular. (= it was written)

## Examples
- Having finished his homework, he went out to play. = Tendo terminado a lição de casa, ele saiu para brincar.
- Not having eaten all day, I was starving. = Sem ter comido o dia todo, eu estava morrendo de fome.
