**was / were + verb-ing** = something was in progress at a moment in the past. It often goes with the past simple:

> I **was taking** a shower **when** the phone **rang**.

The longer action (was taking) was interrupted by the short one (rang).

## Examples
- What were you doing at ten last night?
- It was raining when I left home.
- They were talking about you.

## Quiz
- I ___ sleeping when you called. = was | were | am
- We were ___ TV. = watching | watch | watched
