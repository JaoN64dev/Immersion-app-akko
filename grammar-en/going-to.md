**be going to + verb**:

1. **A plan you already decided**: I'**m going to** visit my grandma this weekend.
2. **A prediction from what you can see**: Look at those clouds. It'**s going to** rain.

In American speech it often sounds like **gonna**: I'm **gonna** sleep. (Don't write it in formal English.)

> For a decision made right now, use **will**: "The phone's ringing." "I'**ll** get it."

## Examples
- I'm going to study abroad next year.
- It's going to snow tonight.
- Are you going to watch the game?

## Quiz
- We ___ going to move next month. = are | is | will
- I'm going to ___ a new laptop. = buy | buying | bought
