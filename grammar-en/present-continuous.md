detect: am|is|are|'m|'re|'s + not|n't + [ing]

**be + verb-ing** says something is happening **now**, or around now.

- I'**m eating** lunch.
- She **isn't watching** TV.
- **Are** you **listening**?

Making -ing: make → mak**ing**, run → ru**nning**, lie → l**ying**.

> It's also used for plans: I'**m meeting** her tomorrow.
> State verbs (know, like, want, need) usually don't take -ing: I **know** him. (not *I'm knowing*)

## Examples
- I'm watching a movie right now.
- It is raining outside.
- They're playing soccer in the park.

## Quiz
- Listen! The baby ___ . = is crying | cries | crying
- What ___ you doing? = are | do | is
