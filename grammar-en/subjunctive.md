After verbs like **suggest, recommend, insist, demand** and adjectives like **essential, important**, the verb in the *that* part stays **plain**, even with he / she. This is especially common in American English.

- I **suggest that** he **see** a doctor. (not *sees*)
- It's **essential that** everyone **be** on time. (not *is*)

## Examples
- The doctor recommended that she rest for a week.
- It is important that he be told the truth.

## Quiz
- I suggest that he ___ earlier. = leave | leaves | left
