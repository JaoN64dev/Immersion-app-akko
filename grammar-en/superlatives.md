For "the most" in a group of three or more: **the + superlative**.

| adjective | superlative |
|---|---|
| short | tall → **the** tall**est**, big → **the** big**gest**, happy → **the** happ**iest** |
| long | **the most** beautiful |
| irregular | good → **the best**, bad → **the worst** |

The group: **in** (a place or group) / **of** (a number): the tallest **in** my class, the best **of** all.

## Examples
- Denali is the highest mountain in North America.
- That was the best day of my life.
- She is the most popular singer in the country.

## Quiz
- It's ___ restaurant in town. (good) = the best | the goodest | better
- This is the ___ difficult question. = most | more | much
