**have / has + past participle** links the past to **now**.

1. **Experience**: I **have been** to Hawaii. / **Have** you **ever seen** it?
2. **Just finished, with a result now**: I'**ve just finished** my homework.
3. **Still true**: We'**ve lived** here **for** ten years / **since** 2015.

> If you say *when*, use the past simple: ~~I have seen him yesterday~~ → I **saw** him yesterday.

Past participles: regular = -ed. Irregular: go → **gone**, see → **seen**, eat → **eaten**, be → **been**.

## Examples
- Have you ever eaten sushi?
- I've already seen this movie.
- She has lived in London for five years.

## Quiz
- I have never ___ to Japan. = been | gone | went
- I ___ him yesterday. = saw | have seen | seen
