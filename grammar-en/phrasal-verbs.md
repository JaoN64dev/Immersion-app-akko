A **phrasal verb** is a verb plus a small word (up, out, off, on…), and the meaning is often quite different. American speech uses them all the time.

| phrasal verb | meaning |
|---|---|
| give up | stop trying |
| find out | discover, learn |
| figure out | understand, solve |
| look forward to | be happy about something coming |
| put off | delay |
| run out (of) | have none left |
| get along (with) | be friendly |
| turn on / off | start / stop a machine |

> Learn them as one word. To look one up, select both words (e.g. *give up*) with the mouse.

## Examples
- Don't give up!
- I'm looking forward to seeing you.
- We ran out of milk.
- I finally figured out the answer.

## Quiz
- (delay) Let's ___ the meeting until Friday. = put off | put on | put up
- (stop trying) Never give ___ ! = up | out | off
