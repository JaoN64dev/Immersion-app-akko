These put the spotlight on one part of a sentence.

- **It is / was … that (who)**: **It was** Tom **who** broke the window. (not someone else)
- **What … is**: **What** I need **is** a long vacation.

Very natural in conversation too: **What I mean is**… / **What I love about** Boston **is**…

## Examples
- It was my sister who told me the news.
- What I want is a little peace and quiet.
- It is the quality that matters, not the price.

## Quiz
- ___ I need is more time. = What | That | It
