---
title: Verbo + -ing o verbo + to (enjoy doing, want to do)
meaning: qué forma va después de un verbo
---

Cuando un verbo va después de otro, el primero decide la forma.

| + -ing | + to + verbo | los dos |
|---|---|---|
| enjoy, finish, mind, stop, keep | want, need, hope, decide, plan | like, love, hate, start |

- I **enjoy cooking**. / I **want to travel**.
- "Quisiera" educado: **I'd like to** + verbo: I**'d like to** order, please.

## Examples
- I really enjoy reading comics. = Me encanta leer cómics.
- She wants to be a doctor. = Ella quiere ser médica.
- I'd like to reserve a table for two. = Quisiera reservar una mesa para dos.
