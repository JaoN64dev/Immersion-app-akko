---
title: Comparativos (bigger than, more … than)
meaning: comparar dos cosas
---

Para comparar dos cosas: **comparativo + than** ("más ... que").

| adjetivo | comparativo |
|---|---|
| corto | tall → tall**er**, big → big**ger**, easy → eas**ier** |
| largo | **more** beautiful, **more** expensive |
| irregular | good → **better**, bad → **worse**, far → **farther / further** |

> Texas is **bigger than** California. / This one is **more expensive than** that one.
> Igual: **as** tall **as** (tan alto como).

## Examples
- My sister is older than me. = Mi hermana es mayor que yo.
- This book is more interesting than the movie. = Este libro es más interesante que la película.
- Today is better than yesterday. = Hoy está mejor que ayer.
