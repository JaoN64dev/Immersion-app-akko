---
title: Third conditional (if I had known, I would have…)
meaning: imaginar un pasado distinto
---

Para imaginar un pasado distinto (a menudo arrepentimiento): nuestro "si hubiera ..., habría ...".

> **If** I **had studied** harder, I **would have passed** the exam.

- Parte del if: **had + participio**
- Resultado: **would have + participio** (o could have / might have)
- Al hablar: If I'**d known**, I'**d have told** you. (suena como "I'd'a told you")

## Examples
- If I had known, I would have helped you. = Si lo hubiera sabido, te habría ayudado.
- If we'd left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight. = Si hubiéramos salido antes, no habríamos perdido el vuelo.
- She would have come if you had invited her. = Ella habría venido si la hubieras invitado.
