---
title: will
meaning: predicciones, decisiones en el momento, promesas
---

**will + verbo**:

1. **Predecir**: It **will** be cold tomorrow.
2. **Decidir en el momento**: I'**ll** call you later.
3. **Promesas y ofrecimientos**: I **won't** tell anyone.
4. **Pedidos**: **Will** you marry me?

Negativa: **won't** (= will not). Al hablar: I'll, you'll, it'll.

## Examples
- I'll help you with your bags. = Te ayudo con las maletas.
- Don't worry, it won't hurt. = No te preocupes, no va a doler.
- I think she will win. = Creo que ella va a ganar.
