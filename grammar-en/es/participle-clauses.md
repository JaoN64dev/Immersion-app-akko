---
title: Oraciones de participio (Having finished…)
meaning: unir ideas con -ing / -ed (formal)
---

Al escribir, se puede quitar el sujeto y la palabra de enlace y empezar con **-ing** o un participio (como nuestro gerundio: "habiendo terminado...").

- **Walking** home, I met an old friend. (= while I was walking)
- **Having finished** the report, she went home. (= after she had finished)
- **Written** in 1950, the book is still popular. (= it was written)

## Examples
- Having finished his homework, he went out to play. = Tras terminar los deberes, salió a jugar.
- Not having eaten all day, I was starving. = Como no había comido en todo el día, me moría de hambre.
