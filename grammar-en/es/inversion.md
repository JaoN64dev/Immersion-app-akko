---
title: Inversión (Never have I…)
meaning: poner una palabra negativa al principio, para dar énfasis
---

Poner una palabra negativa al principio hace que el resto use el **orden de pregunta**. Es formal y enfático, común en textos y discursos.

| normal | invertido |
|---|---|
| I have never seen such a thing. | **Never have I** seen such a thing. |
| He not only sings, but he also dances. | **Not only does he** sing, but he also dances. |
| As soon as I arrived, it started to rain. | **No sooner had I** arrived than it started to rain. |

## Examples
- Never have I been so embarrassed. = Nunca he pasado tanta vergüenza.
- Not only did she win, but she also broke the record. = No solo ganó, sino que además batió el récord.
- Rarely do we see such talent. = Rara vez vemos tanto talento.
