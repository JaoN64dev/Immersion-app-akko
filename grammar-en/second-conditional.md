To imagine something unreal or unlikely: **if + past, would + verb**.

> **If** I **had** a million dollars, I **would buy** a house.

- It's about *now*, but uses the **past**.
- With be, **were** is used for everyone: **If I were** you, I'd say sorry.
- could / might also work: If I had time, I **could** help.

> would + verb is also a polite request: **Would** you **help** me?

## Examples
- If I were you, I would take the job.
- If I knew her number, I'd call her.
- What would you do if you won the lottery?

## Quiz
- If I ___ rich, I would travel the world. = were | am | will be
- If she had a car, she ___ drive to work. = would | will | is
